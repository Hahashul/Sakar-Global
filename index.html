Why I started SAKAR

I didn't read about importing.
I lived every step of it.

My journey didn't start in a boardroom — it started behind a shop counter. From local shops to real estate to nearly a decade inside India's telecom giants — Reliance, Airtel and Tata — I learned how business works from the ground up.

In 2014 I left a settled corporate career to build my own. A 2016 sourcing trip to Guangzhou changed everything: I chose LED lighting and imported my first consignment days before demonetisation — the start of my brand, FG LED Lights.

From finished goods I moved to importing raw material, and built my own 20,000 sq ft manufacturing unit on a 1-acre plot at IT Park, Bhopal — every step from scratch. That end-to-end experience is what I bring to SAKAR Global Consultancy.

"Most import coaching stops at finding a cheap supplier. I've taken the full journey — finished goods, to raw material, to my own factory. That's the difference I bring."Ravi Adwani — Founder, SAKAR Global Consultancy

Our journey

From finished goods to a factory

We launched FG LED Lights by importing finished goods from China. Step by step we moved to importing raw material (SKD/CKD), then built our own assembling unit at IT Park, Bhopal — proof the import-to-manufacturing path is real.

Cost & control

Why we shifted to raw material

Importing finished goods is the easy start — but margins and control are thin. Moving to raw-material import gave us better costs, tighter quality control, and the foundation to manufacture in India.

From scratch

Building a brand with limited funds

Our first consignment landed just days before demonetisation. With no business family and limited capital, we built FG LED Lights into a working brand — the same playbook we now run with clients.
